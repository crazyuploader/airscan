## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Project scaffold: Compose app shell, build setup, formatting and CI.
- Access screen that explains and requests precise location and phone state, and handles the approximate choice.
- Mobile tab with per-SIM signal, cell identity, band, 5G SA or NSA, and neighbour cells.
- Wi-Fi tab with connection details, a scan list that respects the 4 scans per 2 minutes limit, and a channel graph.
- Signal meter with a gauge and a live graph. Colour, bars and a quality word show the level.
- History tab, and a foreground recording service that writes CSV and GPX logs.
- Appearance setting (System, Light, Dark, Black) with a pure black theme for AMOLED screens.
- Landscape and wide layouts: a side rail, and multi-column lists.
- Haptic feedback with a switch, and a blurred placeholder card where a missing permission hides details.
- Neighbour cells show the 8 strongest with a "Show all" button, and 2G and 3G cells show their channel.
- A soft strip under the status bar, and readable log file names.
